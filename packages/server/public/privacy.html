Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026 · Version 2.5

This policy explains which personal data Recall processes, why, how long we keep it and what your rights are. Recall is memory for AI agents: it stores what you or your software send it, so an agent can remember people across conversations.

Who we are

Recall is a trade name of Smidt Beheer, Binnendelta 11M, 1261 WZ Blaricum, the Netherlands (Chamber of Commerce/KvK 99124254, VAT NL004444074B96). In this policy "Recall", "we" and "us" mean Smidt Beheer.

For anything about privacy, email privacy@recallmem.dev, or write to the address above. We have not appointed a data protection officer.

Our two roles

  • Controller. We decide how and why we process the data needed to run our business: account, sign-in, billing, usage, website and security data, and our emails with you. This policy is about that data.
  • Processor. Our customers send Recall content, usually about their own users: messages, files and the memory Recall builds from them. For that content the customer is the controller, and we process it only on their instructions, under our data processing agreement (DPA).

If you use an app or agent that is built on Recall, the company behind that app decides what happens to your data. Please contact them first. If you contact us, we pass your request on to them.

Content you send

Recall stores the messages and files you send and builds memory from them. All of it is stored in our database in Frankfurt (EU). To read messages, build memory and answer questions, Recall sends text to AI models through OpenRouter (see who receives data). We look at customer content only when that is needed to give support you ask for, to investigate abuse or a security incident, or when the law requires it.

For this content you are the controller and we are your processor, under the DPA. You need a lawful basis for it and must tell the people it is about.

Plugins and integrations

The Recall plugins and integrations (for example for Claude Code, Codex, OpenClaw and Hermes) send your prompts and the agent's replies to Recall so it can learn, and load what Recall knows at the start of a session. They send this only to the Recall server you configure (api.recallmem.dev by default). Your API key stays on your computer: Claude Code keeps it in your system's credential store, and the other integrations read it from an environment variable or a configuration file you control. The plugins have a setting to turn recording off, and you can delete stored data at any time.

Who receives personal data

These service providers process data for us:

  • Vercel Inc. (USA) hosts the website, the dashboard and the API. The API and dashboard run in Frankfurt (EU); the website is served from Vercel's worldwide network.
  • Neon (USA) runs our database, in Frankfurt (EU).
  • OpenRouter, Inc. (USA) passes our requests to AI models. The model providers it uses process the text to build memory and answer questions: currently OpenAI (USA), which also computes embeddings, and Anthropic (USA).
  • Resend sends sign-in links and account emails. It receives your email address and the email, not your content.
  • Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. (Ireland) handles payments, invoices and tax calculation. Stripe also processes some payment data as a controller in its own right, for example to prevent fraud; see Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare provides DNS for our domains. It does not receive the content of your requests.

We also share personal data, where needed, with our email provider (for messages to support@recallmem.dev), our accountant or tax adviser (billing data, for our bookkeeping), authorities and courts when the law requires it, and a buyer or successor of Recall's business, who must respect this policy.

International transfers

Our database and the servers that run the API and dashboard are in the EU. Some providers are based in, or process data in, the United States: OpenRouter and the model providers it uses (such as OpenAI), and, depending on the service, Vercel, Neon, Resend and companies of the Stripe group.

The European Commission has decided that the United States protects personal data adequately only for companies certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. For each transfer we rely on that certification where the provider has it, and otherwise on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, with additional measures where needed. You can ask us for a copy of these safeguards at support@recallmem.dev.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • rectification of data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • erasure of your data;
  • restriction of processing, for example while we check a complaint;
  • data portability: receive your data in a machine-readable format, or have it sent to another provider;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it, without affecting processing before you withdrew.

Much of this is built in. Account owners and admins can download everything an account stores as a JSON file in the dashboard (Settings), and the owner can delete the whole account there. The API, SDKs and CLI can erase a person (DELETE /v3/workspaces/{workspace}/peers/{peer}), a session or a workspace. For anything else, email support@recallmem.dev. We answer within one month. If a request is complex, we may take two more months and will tell you why. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

You can also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or to the authority in the EU country where you live or work.

Automated decisions

We do not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing. Some abuse checks run automatically, such as refusing sign-up with a disposable email address or not giving welcome credit to a network that received it recently or to an email address that already received it. You can still buy credit and use Recall, and you can ask a person to review the decision at support@recallmem.dev.

Cookies

The website (recallmem.dev) sets no cookies and loads no analytics, tracking or third-party scripts or fonts.

The dashboard (app.recallmem.dev) sets two cookies that are strictly necessary for it to work. They need no consent and are never used for tracking.

Security

We protect data with measures that suit the risk, such as encryption in transit, hashed keys and tokens, and strict separation between accounts. See our security page for details and for how to report a vulnerability.

Children

Recall is a service for businesses and developers. It is not meant for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data as a controller. Customers who process data about children through Recall must meet the GDPR's rules on children's consent.

Changes

We update this policy when our processing changes, and change the date and version at the top. We announce material changes to account owners by email before they take effect. Earlier versions are available on request.
