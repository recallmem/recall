8. Prepaid credit, prices and payment

  • Prepaid credit. Recall is paid with prepaid credit in US dollars. Usage is deducted from your balance as it happens, at the prices on our pricing page at that moment. Learning from messages is charged per million message tokens, with a minimum per message. Storing a message that no reasoning call reads (for example in a session with reasoning off), and storing a fact you add yourself, is charged per million tokens, with the same minimum per item. Chat is charged per query, by reasoning level. Context, search, summaries and background consolidation (the "night shift") are currently included at no extra charge.
  • Taxes. Prices exclude VAT and other taxes. Where VAT applies, Stripe Checkout adds it and shows the total before you pay. Business customers can enter a VAT number at Checkout; VAT is then handled as the law requires, for example under the reverse-charge rule.
  • Top-ups. You buy credit with a top-up, currently between $10 and $1,000 each, paid through Stripe. Each top-up is a separate purchase with its own invoice. The credit is added as soon as the payment is confirmed. When you buy credit, you ask us to make it available immediately (see section 10 if you are a consumer).
  • Auto top-up. Auto top-up is optional. When you switch it on, you authorise us and Stripe to charge the card you saved at Checkout, without you being present, for the amount you chose, each time your balance falls below the threshold you set, until you switch it off. Your bank may ask you to confirm a payment. Each automatic top-up is invoiced. If a charge fails, we try again later; after three failures in a row we switch auto top-up off and email you. You can change it or switch it off in the dashboard at any time.
  • When your balance runs out. Messages you send are still stored, but learning and chat pause until you top up. Usage that was already running when your balance reached zero is still charged, so your balance can go slightly below zero; the next top-up covers that. You can also set a monthly spending cap.
  • No expiry. Purchased credit does not expire while your account is open.
  • Welcome credit. New self-serve accounts may receive free welcome credit (currently $5). It is limited (for example one per network per 30 days, a daily total, and none for disposable email addresses), has no cash value, and cannot be refunded or transferred. We may take back welcome credit obtained through abuse, and may change or end the offer at any time.
  • Price changes. We may change our prices. We announce increases at least 30 days in advance, by email to account owners and on the pricing page; they apply only to usage after that date. If you do not agree, you may stop using Recall and get your unused purchased credit refunded (section 9). Price decreases may apply immediately.
  • Payment problems. If a payment is reversed or charged back, we may remove the related credit and suspend your account until the matter is resolved.
