Support

Last updated: 9 October 2026 · Version 2.0

Email

Questions, problems or feedback: support@recallmem.dev. Include your account email and, for API problems, the request id from the response. We aim to answer within two working days.

Setup guides for the API, SDKs, MCP and every plugin are in the docs.

Contact point for users and authorities

Under the EU Digital Services Act, support@recallmem.dev is our single point of contact for users of the service and for authorities of the EU member states, the European Commission and the European Board for Digital Services. You can write to us in English or Dutch. Every message is read by a person.

Report illegal content

If you believe Recall stores illegal content, email support@recallmem.dev with "Illegal content notice" in the subject. The terms of service explain what a notice should contain and how we handle it.

Privacy requests

To access, correct, export or delete your personal data, email privacy@recallmem.dev, or use the tools described in the privacy policy. If your data reached Recall through an app built on it, please contact the company behind that app first.

Withdraw from a top-up

Consumers can withdraw from a credit top-up within 14 days and get the unused part refunded. Email support@recallmem.dev; see section 10 of the terms for the details and a model withdrawal form.

Security reports

Please report vulnerabilities privately to security@recallmem.dev. Our security page explains what to include and our safe-harbour terms for good-faith research.

Plugin issues

Bugs in the plugins can also be reported on GitHub. Issues there are public: do not post API keys, personal data or security vulnerabilities.
